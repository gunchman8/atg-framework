# ATGF One Click Start

## For users who do not know AI agents

You only need to do this:

1. Copy the ATGF repository link.
2. Paste it into your AI assistant.
3. Tell the AI what you want to build.

Example:

> I want to build a sales application. Use ATGF to create an AI team for this project.

The AI should then:

1. Understand the goal.
2. Create suitable agent roles.
3. Define permissions.
4. Recommend model capabilities.
5. Explain the workflow.
6. Ask for approval before execution.

## Important

ATGF does not mean giving AI unlimited control.

It creates an organized AI team with:

- clear responsibilities;
- limited permissions;
- human approval checkpoints.

Think of it as hiring an AI company team instead of asking one employee to do everything.
