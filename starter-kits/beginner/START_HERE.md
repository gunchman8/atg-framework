# ATGF Beginner Starter Kit

Welcome to ATGF (Agent Team Governance Framework).

You do not need to understand AI agents, GitHub, or coding first.

Think of AI like a company:

- Planner: makes the plan
- Builder: creates the solution
- Reviewer: checks mistakes
- Tester: validates results

ATGF helps you organize AI work with:

- clear roles
- clear permissions
- review steps
- human approval before risky actions

## How to use

1. Open your AI coding assistant.
2. Provide the ATGF repository link.
3. Use the bootstrap prompt.
4. Describe what you want to build.
5. Review the proposed team before execution.

The goal is not to give AI unlimited power.

The goal is to give AI the right responsibility and boundaries.
